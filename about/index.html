About Reintjes Lawn Care

Owner-operated, Mission Hills-based, and built around one idea: every lawn should look better after we leave than the one next door.

Who We Are

Founded by George Reintjes

Reintjes Lawn Care was founded in 2024 by George Reintjes, based in Mission Hills, KS. What started as a single route has grown into a business serving five communities across the Kansas City metro, still run the same way it started: one owner, one crew, and a direct line to the person doing the work.

Call or text (970) 390-5906 and you're talking to George, not a call center. That's on purpose. It's how estimates stay accurate and how small requests, like an extra pass around the mailbox or trimming that one overgrown hedge, actually get handled.
